Not mainly. It’s an entertainment channel now — vlogs, podcasts, talk shows and challenges with friends. Gaming still pops up when the crew wants to play.

How often do uploads happen?

Regularly, with a mix of vlogs, Krissy Talks episodes, challenges and shorts.

Yes. Use the Ask Krissy section to pitch a challenge, a vlog spot, a podcast topic — or send a story or a problem you want advice on.

Yes — guests, creators and friends of friends are welcome. Reach out through the contact section.

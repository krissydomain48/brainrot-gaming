What is Krissy Talks?

Krissy Talks is a comedy podcast from the Brainrot Krissy channel. In the Worst Life Advice episodes, viewers' real problems get the most questionable answers possible — please don't actually follow any of it. Other episodes are just two brothers talking: country music versus Justin Bieber, buffalo boys instead of cowboys, and what life in a Nepali village taught us.

Got a problem you want the worst possible advice on — or a real story you want heard? Send it through Ask Krissy. You choose if it's shared and whether your name is used.
